A running collection of books, articles, and resources worth keeping around. Updated as I read.

## Books

### Currently Reading

**The Master and Margarita** by Mikhail Bulgakov

A Soviet-era novel where the Devil visits Moscow and chaos follows. Philosophical, satirical, and unlike anything else. The kind of book that makes you think differently about power, faith, and human nature.

### Finished and Recommended

**System Design Interview (Vol. 1)** by Alex Xu

The clearest practical introduction to designing large-scale systems. Rate limiters, consistent hashing, distributed caches — explained without assuming you already know everything. A genuine reference I return to.

### To Read

**Designing Data-Intensive Applications** by Martin Kleppmann

The book on distributed systems. Replication, partitioning, consistency models. If you build backend systems, this one comes up constantly.

**The Pragmatic Programmer** by David Thomas and Andrew Hunt

On the craft of writing software that lasts. Keeps appearing on every list for a reason.
