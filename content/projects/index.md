## Customer Behavior Analysis
**Rutgers University · MS Computer Science**

Implemented and compared clustering algorithms — KMeans, OPTICS, and DBSCAN — to identify shopping activity patterns from a retail dataset. Analyzed correlating customer attributes and visualized cluster formations to evaluate algorithm performance across varying data densities. Concluded that OPTICS outperformed the others on datasets with non-uniform point density due to its adaptive density estimation.

**Tech:** Python · Scikit-learn · Matplotlib · Pandas
